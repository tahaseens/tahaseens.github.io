Overview

PublicBrief helps residents understand upcoming local-government decisions involving public money, surveillance, land use, infrastructure, and community services. It turns dense agendas, staff reports, and public notices into clear, source-grounded briefs before a hearing, contract award, or final vote.

The politically neutral prototype focuses on Loudoun County, Virginia. It identifies what is being proposed, who holds each decision-making role, what information may be missing, and how residents can participate—without recommending a political position.
